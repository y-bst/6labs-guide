6labs Studio

Guides

How 6labs Studio works, one flow at a time. There are two modes, and they answer two different questions.

Intelligence

What are my players actually doing?

Intelligence agents have already watched your gameplay, so you can ask them about it in plain words. They answer with the sessions they read, and they get sharper the more your studio gives them.

Two agents, and what they know about your game.

Testing

Is anything wrong with the build?

Testing agents look at your game and tell you what is broken, confusing or unfinished. Real players record sessions, or 6labs AI players play the build themselves. Either way, every finding points at the moment in the video that shows it.

Four testing agents, and the library they read from.
