Guide 01 · Human testing

Gameplay Library

Before any human test can run, the studio needs gameplay recordings. This guide shows how videos get into 6labs Studio and how they're organised — so tests can use them.

Scroll to start

One home for every gameplay video

The Gameplay Library is a page in the Testing mode of 6labs Studio. Every gameplay video the studio has lives here.

  • Comes in from: the 6labs Gameplay Recorder App, browser upload and CLI
  • Not here: BlueStacks recordings and SDK videos

Three ways to bring videos in

  • Gameplay Recorder App — records testers while they play, then uploads
  • Browser upload — drop in videos you already have
  • CLI — push hundreds of videos from a computer

Next, each one up close.

Set it up once

Install the 6labs Gameplay Recorder App on the PC testers play on, then enter:

  • Work email — optional
  • App ID — tells the app which game to record
  • Region — whether you're in mainland China

Click Save & Start.

PC appAndroid & iOS coming soon

It waits for the game

The app shows Waiting for the game. When the tester launches the game, recording starts on its own — no button to press.

Pause stops recording until they resume.

Testers just play

While the game runs, the app shows REC and a timer. The tester plays as normal.

Close the game — it uploads

When the tester closes the game, recording stops and the video starts uploading on its own.

To stop early, they can click Stop recording: Stop & upload sends it, Stop & discard throws it away.

The video uploads itself

The app uploads the session and shows its progress.

Once it's done, the video appears in the Gameplay Library with a Recorder app badge.

Click Upload videos

On the Gameplay Library page, click Upload videos in the top-right corner.

Drop in files, add tags

Drop videos in or click to browse. Tags are optional and apply to every video in the upload.

MP4 · MOV · WEBM · AVI · MKVMax 500 MB eachUp to 50 per upload

Upload hundreds from the command line

For big batches, open Upload videos → Bulk via CLI and follow three steps:

  • Install the 6labs CLI
  • Log in — authorises the computer in the browser
  • Upload a folder with tags, game name and App ID

It runs in the background, retries failures and resumes interrupted uploads.

Every video lands in one library

Gameplay Recorder App, browser upload and CLI all send videos to the same place — the Gameplay Library.

What a video card shows

Every video, from any source, becomes a card like this:

  • Source — where the video came from
  • Username — who added it
  • System tag — added automatically, can't be edited
  • Your tags — labels you add, up to 5
  • Edit & delete — appear when you hover

Tag many videos at once

Tick the videos you want. A card appears at the bottom:

  • Add tag — adds a tag to every selected video
  • Delete — removes them together

Find the right batch

Three ways to narrow the list:

  • Tags — tap one or more, like Build V2.2. Last 24h shows new videos.
  • Source — Recorder app, CLI or browser upload
  • Search — by file name or tag

Tests pick their videos from here

To analyse videos, you open a test such as User Test. It lets you pick videos straight from this library.

Upload filesBulk via CLI

Uploading hundreds of clips? Push them straight from your machine with the 6labs CLI — it runs in the background, retries on failure, and resumes interrupted uploads.

1 · Install
npm install -g @6labs/cli
2 · AuthenticateOpens your browser to authorize this machine.
6labs login
3 · Upload a folder
6labs videos upload ./gameplay/*.mp4 \
  --tags "Build V2.2,onboarding" \
  --game-name "Don't Let it Die" \
  --app-id "10007" \
  --concurrency 4

Globs like ./gameplay/**/*.mp4 upload nested folders. --concurrency controls parallel uploads. Clips appear in the library as each one finishes.

Say it to a client

  • Your testers just play — recording starts on its own.
  • Already have recordings? Upload them, or push hundreds with one command.
  • Tag by build or feature, and pull the right batch in one tap.

Questions clients ask

Do testers have to press record?

No. Recording starts when the game launches and uploads when they close it. They can also stop and upload manually.

Does the recorder work on phones?

Not yet. The 6labs Gameplay Recorder App is for PC. Android and iOS are coming soon.

Can we use recordings we already have?

Yes. Upload up to 50 at a time in the browser, or any number with the CLI.

Which video formats work?

MP4, MOV, WEBM, AVI and MKV, up to 500 MB each.

Do BlueStacks or SDK recordings show up here?

No. The library holds videos from the Gameplay Recorder App, browser uploads and the CLI.
